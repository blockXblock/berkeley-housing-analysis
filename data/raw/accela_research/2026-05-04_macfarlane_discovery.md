## Headline Finding

Located the 4th building of Acheson Commons / Modera at **2111 University Ave**
(Building D / MacFarlane, permit B2015-03005, APN 057-2046-010-00, 68 units).
This reconciles the 4 buildings to UP2010-0078's 205-unit total.

The MacFarlane building was not at a Walnut St address as initially suspected —
it's at the corner of University Ave and Shattuck Ave, with secondary address
1987 Shattuck Ave.
