Does the institution integrate sustainability information into the educational offerings or materials provided during new student orientation or the equivalent?:
Yes


Narrative outlining how sustainability information is integrated into new student orientation or the equivalent:

During Golden Bear Orientation (GBO), newly admitted first-year and transfer students can hear from sustainability departments around campus as part of the campus resource fair. The departments inform students about sustainability activities, programs and resources around campus.

All new undergraduates have to complete an on-line orientation, Golden Bear Prep, that includes information on campus sustainability: https://orientation.berkeley.edu/before-you-arrive/golden-bear-prep The module "Getting to Know Campus & Bay Area" provides students an orientation on UC Berkeley's sustainable campus. Resources are shared from the The Student Environmental Resource Center (SERC), Office of Sustainability, and Housing & Dining Sustainability. Students are also educated on proper waste sorting on campus and tested on their environmental knowledge with a short quiz.

At GBO, Cal Zero Waste works with Golden Bear Orientation organizers to move the orientation towards being a zero waste event and provide zero waste training and resources to GBO Orientation Leaders during their Orientation Leader training sessions. The training included information about the UC Zero Waste goal and basic information on how incoming students can contribute to bringing the campus closer to that goal as a resident and also as a student. The goal is to have Orientation Leaders integrate the zero waste messaging into their campus tours and conversations with incoming students during Golden Bear Orientation as well as into the activities that are planned.

Graduate students participate in orientations as well that include the GradFest, a collaborative student activities and resource fair that includes the Office of Sustainability and the Student Environmental Resource Center. The orientation is paperless – students can download The GradBook on the UC Berkeley Events App to get access to the NGSO schedule, handouts, and graduate events year-round. http://grad.berkeley.edu/news/headlines/arrive-orient-and-go/



Does the institution integrate sustainability information into the educational offerings or materials provided during new employee orientation or the equivalent?:
Yes


Narrative outlining how sustainability information is integrated into new employee orientation of the equivalent:

All incoming UC Berkeley staff receive a briefing on campus sustainability options at employee orientation sessions. They are also offered opportunities to practice sustainability through committees, via newsletters and through social media channels. The campus also offers energy savings campaigns and sustainability learning events.

UC Berkeley provides an introduction to alternative transportation as part of their outreach for New Employee Reception & Orientation (NERO). Parking & Transportation shares the benefits of taking alternative transit as an effective substitute to driving alone.

Everyone, faculty and staff, interacts with Parking & Transportation as a new employee to find commute options, and the employee orientation offers information and sign-ups on the commute alternative programs. Among the sustainable traveling options featured, Parking & Transportation highlights Bear Transit and public transit options such as AC Transit and BART for Bay Area travel. The presentation also encourages bike commuting by highlighting UCB's Gold Level Status as a Bike-friendly University, ample bike parking on campus, secure bike cages, and bikeshare programs. Sustainable transport is also featured with BerkeleyMoves!, UCB's official RideMatching & Trip Planning Platform. BerkeleyMoves! is exclusive to the Berkeley community and offers carpool matching & prize incentives for calendar entries. Lastly, Golden Bear Trip Planning is a personalize planning service offered to the Berkeley community to identify ideal and sustainable travel options for your trip.

Zero Waste, The Office of Sustainability, and Environmental Health and Safety are featured under additional resources. The NERO virtual scavenger hunt also features the Office of Sustainability.



Does the institution have dashboards and/or signage highlighting the institution’s sustainability features or performance?:
Yes


Description of the institution’s sustainability dashboards and/or signage:

UC Berkeley has dashboards and signage highlighting its sustainability features and performance. The university uses various platforms and communication tools to share sustainability data and progress. These include:

CalCAP Dashboard: UC Berkeley's Climate Action Plan (CalCAP) has an interactive dashboard displaying greenhouse gas emissions data, energy consumption, and progress towards carbon neutrality goals.

Energy and Water Dashboards: There are several real-time energy monitoring dashboards that showcase electricity, water usage, and other resource data to students and staff.

Sustainability Signage: The campus also features physical signage in key locations, such as green-certified buildings, recycling stations, and energy-efficient areas, to inform the community about sustainability initiatives.

Annual Sustainability Reports: UC Berkeley releases sustainability reports and updates on its website, sharing key metrics on waste diversion, water conservation, and greenhouse gas emissions.

Has the institution coordinated one or more sustainability-focused outreach campaigns during the previous three years?:
Yes


Description of sustainability-focused outreach campaigns from the previous three years:
 

UC Berkeley has coordinated several sustainability-focused outreach campaigns over the past three years, with various initiatives aimed at engaging the campus community in environmental stewardship and social justice. Some of the key campaigns include:

Zero Waste Campaigns: UC Berkeley has promoted zero waste through initiatives like the Zero Waste by 2020 goal, focusing on waste reduction, composting, recycling, and minimizing single-use plastics. Campus-wide events and partnerships with student organizations help educate the community on waste sorting.

Earth Week Celebrations (April): UC Berkeley hosts annual Earth Week events, featuring workshops, panels, film screenings, and hands-on service projects to highlight sustainability projects and initiatives.

CalMoveOut: This initiative promotes the sustainable disposal of furniture and other items during student move-outs, helping reduce waste and supporting a circular economy.

Cool Campus Challenge: A systemwide UC initiative that encourages students, faculty, and staff to adopt climate-friendly behaviors, focusing on reducing carbon emissions and promoting energy-saving actions. Outreach is done through social media, email campaigns, and workshops.

Clean Air Month (October): UC Berkeley promoted Clean Air Month, advocating for clean air practices and encouraging the campus community to take a Clean Air Pledge. Clean Air Day 2022

Climate Justice Week (October): A key event every October, Climate Justice Week focuses on the intersection of climate action and social justice, featuring workshops, panels, and community engagement activities to address environmental racism and the disproportionate impacts of climate change on vulnerable communities. Climate Justice Week

Sustainable Transportation Initiatives: Through events like the Sustainable Transportation Fair, UC Berkeley promotes alternatives to single-occupancy vehicles, including biking, public transit, and electric vehicle use.

Food Security and Sustainability: In collaboration with the UC Berkeley Basic Needs Center, the university raises awareness about food security and sustainable food practices, supporting campus gardens and sustainable dining hall sourcing.

Green Labs Program: This program encourages more sustainable practices in research labs through energy and water conservation, chemical safety, and waste reduction, with informational sessions and audits to support lab participation.

Description of the methodology used to determine the reach of the institution’s sustainability outreach and communications:

To estimate the percentage of the UC Berkeley community (students, faculty, staff) reached by sustainability-related outreach and communications efforts, several data sources were taken into account.

Campus Communications Platforms:

Email campaigns: Collected data on sustainability newsletters and event invitations sent through campus-wide distribution lists.

Social Media Analytics: Retrieved metrics from official UC Berkeley sustainability accounts (e.g., Facebook, Twitter, Instagram) to capture engagement metrics, including reach, impressions, likes, shares, and comments.

Website Analytics: Analyzed data from the sustainability office’s website, including page views and unique visitors, particularly for high-traffic resources or announcements.

Events and Workshops Attendance: Gathered attendance data from in-person and virtual sustainability events, seminars, and workshops, such as the Sustainable Travel Fair and any training sessions.

Survey Data:

Annual Sustainability Survey: Conducted a survey among a representative sample of students, faculty, and staff to measure awareness and engagement with sustainability communications and events.

Event Feedback Surveys: Included questions about communications effectiveness to understand how attendees learned about events and their engagement level.
