8.1 Publicly shared facilities
Does the institution have on-site space for meetings or events that is publicly accessible?:
Yes


Does the institution provide free or low cost access to the meeting or event space?:
Yes


Description of the institution’s publicly accessible space for meetings or events, including any free or low cost access options:

UC Berkeley has several on-site spaces for meetings and events that are publicly accessible. Some of the key venues include:

Martin Luther King Jr. Student Union: Offers a variety of meeting rooms and event spaces, including Pauley Ballroom and other multipurpose rooms. These spaces are available for both campus and external organizations to rent for events and conferences.

Alumni House: A centrally located venue on campus, available for public rental. It offers meeting rooms and event spaces for conferences, workshops, receptions, and other gatherings.

International House: Provides several event spaces, including the Chevron Auditorium, for public use. These facilities are frequently rented out for conferences, lectures, and social events.

David Brower Center: While technically off-campus, the David Brower Center is closely affiliated with UC Berkeley and provides environmentally friendly meeting and event spaces that are open to the public.

 These facilities ensure that UC Berkeley is accessible not just to the university community, but also to the broader public for meetings, conferences, and events.



Does the institution have at least one on-site facility with services or programs to help people meet their basic needs and is publicly accessible?:
Yes


Does the institution provide free or low cost access to the basic needs facilities?:
Yes


Description of the institution’s publicly accessible facilities that help people meet their basic needs, including any free or low cost access options:

Berkeley Food Pantry: Provides free food to students, staff, and community members facing food insecurity. The pantry is open to all UC Berkeley students, faculty, and staff, and some services are extended to the local community. Basic Needs Center (BNC): Located on campus, the BNC offers comprehensive services to address food, housing, and financial insecurity. It includes a food pantry, support for housing resources, and emergency grants. While it primarily serves students, many services are open to the public or involve community partnerships. Tang Center (University Health Services): Provides healthcare services to students, faculty, and staff, with certain services open to the public. The center offers low-cost health services, including urgent care, mental health support, and wellness programs.



Does the institution have at least one on-site facility that provides cultural services and is publicly accessible?:
Yes


Does the institution provide free or low cost access to the cultural service facilities?:
Yes


Description of the institution’s publicly accessible facilities that provide cultural services, including any free or low cost access options:

UC Berkeley Art Museum and Pacific Film Archive (BAMPFA) offers a range of cultural services, including art exhibitions, film screenings, and educational programs, and is open to the public. Other cultural facilities on campus include:
The Berkeley Art Studio: Offers workshops and classes in various arts like ceramics and photography, accessible to both students and the public.
The Hearst Museum of Anthropology: Showcases global cultural artifacts and is open to the public, providing an important educational and cultural resource.
The Phoebe A. Hearst Museum of Anthropology: Features exhibitions on diverse cultures and human history, also publicly accessible.



Does the institution have at least one on-site facility that provides recreational services and is publicly accessible?:
Yes


Does the institution provide free or low cost access to the recreational service facilities?:
Yes


Description of the institution’s publicly accessible facilities that provide recreational services, including any free or low cost access options:

Yes, UC Berkeley has on-site recreational facilities that provide services to both the campus community and the public. One key example is the UC Berkeley Recreational Sports Facility (RSF). While it primarily serves students, faculty, and staff, the RSF also offers memberships to the public, giving them access to various recreational services.

Cal Performances event spaces, such as Zellerbach Hall, Zellerbach Playhouse, and Hertz Hall, which are all accessible and serve the campus and community. They also offer more than just physical accessibility: https://calperformances.org/visit/accessibility/
