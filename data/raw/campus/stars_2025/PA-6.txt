6.3 Institutional climate assessment
Has the institution conducted an institutional climate assessment within the previous three years?:
Yes


Description of the methodology used to assess institutional climate:

Climate and engagement surveys have been conducted for staff, faculty and students in the last three years:

Staff engagement surveys: https://hr.berkeley.edu/deib/employee-experience/employee-surveys  Explanation page: https://hr.berkeley.edu/deib/employee-experience/employee-engagement 

Faculty surveys: https://ofew.berkeley.edu/data-and-initiatives/publications-and-reports/faculty-climate-surveys 

Additional student surveys: https://diversity.berkeley.edu/reports-data/survey-results

Each year our HR division administers a survey to all staff to better understand staff needs and experiences. The results are shared to all staff via email and available online. The results are disaggregated by unit and demographics. 

https://calviz.berkeley.edu/t/BRSPublic/views/StateoftheWorkplaceIndicesFeb2024V3/Overview

Administered from March until July of the Spring semester, the UC Undergraduate Experience Survey (UCUES) survey responses are matched with institutional data to provide a detailed portrait of students' background, academic and co-curricular activities, goals and aspirations, experiences with academic and administrative units, self-assessments of gains in academic and social skills, interactions with other students, engagement in community service and civic activities, perceived obstacles to academic success and many other topics.

https://calviz.berkeley.edu/#/site/OPAP/views/UCUES2022Public/TableofContents
