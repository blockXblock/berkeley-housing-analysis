SUSTAINABILITY

 Berkeley has declared a Climate Emergency
 New climate conditions will lead to: severe heatwaves and intense
  precipitation events; major wildland fires; reduced air quality and
  biodiversity; and gradual flooding of coastal areas
 Road systems will need to adapt to this changing environment
 Our shared public investments, policies and actions must work to
  mitigate climate impact and adapt to long-term environmental
  changes, as well as build our resilience to short-term events
