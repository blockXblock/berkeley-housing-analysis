SUMMARY/DISCUSSION:

The Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory (LBNL) is managed and operated by the Regents of
the University of California under contract with the federal government (under what is termed a
"Prime Contract").


As part of its management duties, the Regents of the University of California contracted with the
Alameda County Fire Department (via a "Subcontract'') for fire protection and emergency medical
services for the LBNL. The ꞏcurrent service contract or "Subcontract" between the Alameda
County Fire Department and the Regents of the University of California for these services expires
on December 31, 2022.

On April 29, 2022, LBNL issued an RFP RR 2022, Request for Proposal for Fire Suppression and
Related Services, and ACFD submitted a formal proposal to LBNL on June 8, 2022. On October
6, 2022, LBNL notified ACFD that it was a successful bidder, and both parties began working on
a new Subcontract later that month. At this time, both parties have agreed to enter into Subcontract
No. 767098 for the term of 1/1/23 through 12/31/24, and a fixed compensation amount of
$10,603,985 for fire protection services at LBNL. The Subcontract also includes a not-to-exceed
amount of $36,490 for maintenance and repair services of University furnished federal
government vehicles and air compressor systems. Subcontract provides three one-year options of
extending the term to 12/31/27 which increases total fixed contract amount for fire protection
services to $28,339,455 (an increase of $17,735,470 for the three option years), and an increase of
the total not-to-exceed contract amount for maintenance and repair services of University
furnished federal government vehicles and air compressor systems to $101,374 (an increase of
$64,884 for the three option years).

Staff recommends that the Board of Directors approve the Subcontract No. 7670978 between the
Regents of the University of California and the Alameda County Fire Department for fire
protection services and maintenance and repair services of University furnished federal
government vehicles and air compressor systems at the Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory
and authorize the Fire Chief to sign the Subcontract and subsequent modifications related to the
Subcontract, subject to review and approval as to form by County Counsel.

FINANCING:

There is no impact to County General Fund. LBNL will pay 100% of all direct costs and 3.92%
of shared costs based on ACFD allocation methodology. LBNL fire services contract cost is
incorporated in ACFD's adopted FY 2022-23 MOE budget. Funding for future years will be
included in the future ACFD budget appropriations.
