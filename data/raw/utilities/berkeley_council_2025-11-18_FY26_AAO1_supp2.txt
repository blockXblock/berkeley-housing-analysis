Item Description: Amendment: FY 2026 Annual Appropriations Ordinance

Submitted by:                Maricar Dupaya, Budget Manager

The Budget and Finance Policy Committee (“Committee”) held public meetings on
November 6 and November 13, 2025, to discuss the Amendment to the Fiscal Year
2026 Annual Appropriations Ordinance. At the November 13 meeting, the Committee
continued its discussion of the recommended carryover and adjustments proposed by
the City Manager. The committee approved the City Manager’s recommendations,
including the following new adjustment:

    •   $500,000: Proposition 47 Cohort 5 Grant Award from the Board of State and
        Community Corrections. Resolution: No. 71,963–N.S.

In addition, the committee reviewed the General Fund Unassigned Fund Balance
calculations. Fiscal policies state that any General Fund investment interest earned
above the established $6 million baseline is to be allocated in equal one-third
increments to the Section 115 Trust, General Fund Reserves, and to Capital. The policy
would allocate approximately $2.8 million to each category.

The Committee made the following recommendations:
   • $2,822,392: Following the fiscal policy of transferring investment interest above
      baseline of $6 million to Sec.115 Trust Fund.
   • $2,822,392: Following the fiscal policy of transferring investment interest above
      baseline of $6 million to Capital.
   • $2,822,392: Following the fiscal policy of transferring investment interest above
      baseline of $6 million to General Fund Stabilization Reserves Fund at 55% and
      Catastrophic Reserves at 45%.
