# 2025 APR Data Collection Checklist

**Purpose**: Collect permit milestone dates from Berkeley Accela portal to generate the 2025 HCD Annual Progress Report.

**APR Deadline**: April 1, 2026 (for calendar year 2025)

**Data Entry Portal**: https://aca-prod.accela.com/BERKELEY/

---

## Instructions

For each project below:
1. Go to https://aca-prod.accela.com/BERKELEY/
2. Click "Search" → "Building" or "Planning"
3. Enter the permit number (e.g., ZP2024-0162) or street address
4. Record all milestone dates that occurred in 2025

## Alternative Data Collection Methods

### 1. Building Eye Bulk Download
1. Go to https://berkeley.buildingeye.com/
2. Set date filter: Jan 1, 2025 - Dec 31, 2025
3. Filter by permit type: Building, Planning
4. Click "Download CSV"
5. Save as: `data/reference/building_eye_2025.csv`

### 2. Request from City of Berkeley
Contact: Planning Department
Email: planning@berkeleyca.gov
Request: "2025 housing entitlements and building permits for APR reporting"

### 3. Berkeley Open Data Portal
Check: https://data.cityofberkeley.info/
Look for: Permit datasets with 2025 records
