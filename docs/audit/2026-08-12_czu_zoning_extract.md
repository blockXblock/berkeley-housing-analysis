## THE load-bearing finding (line 817)
> Berkeley zoning "does not contain a minimum or maximum density standard expressed in units per acre for the majority of its districts... the zones have no density caps."

**Implication:** Berkeley residential zoning is **form-based (FAR / height / lot-area-per-unit)**, NOT du/ac-capped (R-2A's 26 du/ac and R-1/R-2 lot-area-per-unit are the exceptions). So "actual vs allowed" must be computed on **FAR + height**, not du/ac, for R-3/R-4/commercial.

## Policy context (AFFH, lines 765–789)
City commits to upzone "the highest resource and higher income neighborhoods... Solano, north Shattuck, and College" to balance "formerly red-lined areas" — **≥2,000 units on corridors by Dec 2026**.
