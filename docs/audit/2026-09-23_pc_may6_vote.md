## The load-bearing finding

**The Commission rejected staff's Option 1A and chose Option 1B.** That is not a detail.

- **Option 1A (staff recommendation, rejected)** = *"a defined set of specific parcels"* —
  a fixed, named map.
- **Option 1B (adopted)** = **objective criteria**: minimum 7,000 sf lot, minimum 80 ft depth,
  corner location, **absence of existing residential units**, not a designated historic
  resource. Any parcel meeting all of them qualifies.

So after 6 May 2026 **nobody names parcels.** Qualification is a measurable property of a lot,
not a choice made about a business. Any description of the proposal as "these three sites" is
describing **Option 1A — the version the Commission voted down.**

## On the Nabolom question

Checked across all 27 published CZU documents plus the four Commission minutes:

- **"Nabolom" appears in ZERO City documents.** It appears only on WECAN's College Avenue page,
  which names the Webster post-office lot, the 7-Eleven strip mall at Russell, and the
  Nabolom / Five Little Monkeys corner as "three sites."
- **The Wells Fargo lot was never substituted out.** Section 14 of the May 6 amendments rezones
  *both* the USPS Parking Lot and the Wells Fargo Parking Lot from R-2 to C-E.
- **No commissioner is recorded proposing any site swap**, in February, March or May. February
  was a discussion item (votes only on minutes, chair election, postponing Adeline, adjournment);
  March's corridors item produced a staff direction with no recorded motion.

**A private group naming a specific named business as a redevelopment site is therefore its own
characterisation, not the City's** — and under the adopted Option 1B it does not even match the
mechanism, which names nobody.

## A time-sensitivity nobody has flagged

The consultant's category **D. Very Low / No redevelopment potential** explicitly includes
*"Sites with Thriving Businesses."* Business health is an input to the grade.

**So a struggling business makes its own parcel MORE likely to be classified as developable,
not less.** The same logic runs through Option 1B's "absence of existing residential units."
The classification is a snapshot of 2025 trading conditions, and it moves. Any published map
of "opportunity sites" needs that stated on its face, or it will be read as permanent when it
is nothing of the kind.
