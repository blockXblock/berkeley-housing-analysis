UC Berkeley New Student Housing

Four major UC Berkeley student housing projects in the pipeline: 1950 Oxford (300 units), 2200 Bancroft (550 units), 2556 Haste (556 units), and the Anna Head site at 2400 Bowditch (750 units / ~1,500 beds). Together these projects represent over 5,000 new students housed. The tour starts at the Campanile and visits each in turn. Buildings shown at proposed heights and footprints. Purple: UC Berkeley; Yellow, Orange: Approved, Permitted; Blue: Under Construction; Green: Completed.

Reading

Maintenance: Of Everything (Part One) — Stewart Brand (Stripe Press, January 2026). Brand's new book argues that the real work of civilization isn't invention but the patient care of complex systems. "Civilization is maintenance."

How Buildings Learn: What Happens After They're Built — Stewart Brand. Brand shows how buildings change over time as their users adapt, hack, and retrofit them, often in ways designers never anticipated. It's an essential primer for anyone designing civic systems — digital or physical — who wants to build things that are flexible, humane, and responsive to real-world use over decades. The intellectual home for what berkeleybuild.com does: independently following how Berkeley changes over time. How new housing actually moves from proposal to design to entitlement to construction to deep impacts on our community, our daily conversations, our fabric of life.
