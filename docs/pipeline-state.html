The levers — what could move the stalled states

The record tells us the exposure — how many units sit in each state and for how long. Whether a lever moves them is policy judgment, so every lever wears its tag.

Entitlement shot-clock & extension certainty modeled

Entitled approvals quietly age out: 3,751 units are already lapse-eligible under BMC 23.404.060 and none has been declared lapsed. A standing extension policy with a use-it-or-lose-it horizon makes the waiting room a queue instead of a shelf. Exposure: 6,441 units now in state 2.

Defer city fees to certificate of occupancy modeled

Fees due at permit issuance front-load cost onto the exact moment financing is hardest. Deferral to CO moves the bill to when the building earns. Exposure: 6,441 units now in state 2.

Permit-extension triage on the stalled shelf modeled

Contact every permit older than 18 months with no activity: extend the live ones, retire the dead ones — the register stops hiding both. Exposure: 205 units now in state 3.
