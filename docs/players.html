What this view does not know yet

The money is missing. Construction lenders live on deeds of trust at the County Recorder; investors in LLC filings with the Secretary of State. Neither is loaded yet — this page will say so until they are. Coverage is majors-heavy: the neighborhood fabric's hundreds of small builders and homeowners aren't named in the record.

Who appears here. Everyone named on the record in a professional role — developer, architect, applicant, firm or person — plus owners that are organizations. The 2,294 individual owners (including family trusts) are named in the Explorer and the database rather than drawn here, so the graph shows who builds rather than every household that holds title. Their parcels are shown, as title records, on the ownership map. And a line between two players means they appear on the same project — the record does not know who hired whom.
