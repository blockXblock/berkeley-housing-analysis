What would you like to do today?

The City of Berkeley performs electronic plan review. Initial submittal of plans and other construction documents, in PDF format, must be completed in person at the Permit Service Center. Plans and supporting documents in response to plan review comments may be submitted through this online portal.

To find out today's Scheduled Inspections

  • Click Here

To Schedule an Inspection

  • Search for a Permit below, then click, "Record Info."  Select, "Inspections," and click "Schedule or Request an Inspection."  View step by step Instructions.

        For additional assistance regarding inspections, contact Building and Safety 
        at (510) 981-7440.

To view Permit processing status

  • Search for a Permit below then click, "Record Info" and select "Processing Status."  View step by step Instructions.

To upload revised or additional documents to an existing Permit application under plan check review

  • Log in and find the Permit on your Records list.
  • Click, "Record Info," and select "Attachments."  Use the, "Add," button to upload your documents and click, "Save," to complete.  View step by step Instruction.

        For additional information or further assistance, contact the Permit Service Center 
        at (510) 981-7500.

General Search

 

For permits prior to 2016, search using only the last four digits of the Permit Number and "%" as a placeholder for the rest.

Example: For the Building Permit 14-3744, enter %3744.

You may expand the date settings if the permit was issued more that two years ago.
