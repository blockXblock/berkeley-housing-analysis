**Subject: Public Records Act request — Clariti Cloud Inc. permit-system contract (RFP 24-11661-C)**

Under the California Public Records Act (Gov. Code § 7920.000 et seq.), I request copies of the
following records concerning the City's contract with **Clariti Cloud Inc.** for a Comprehensive
Permit Management System (**RFP Specification No. 24-11661-C**; City Council Item 16, January 20,
2026; total not-to-exceed $5,359,128), replacing the Accela permitting system:

1. The **fully executed contract/agreement** with Clariti Cloud Inc., including all exhibits,
   attachments, schedules, and any amendments or change orders to date.
2. The **Statement of Work (SOW)** and any **implementation plan, project charter, or project
   schedule** for the Permit Software Procurement (PSP) project.
3. The **software subscription/license agreement**, any terms of service, and any
   **data-processing addendum**.
4. Any provisions, exhibits, or technical specifications addressing:
   a. **public data access or open-data APIs** (including any community-facing or developer API);
   b. **data export, data portability, or bulk data extract** capabilities;
   c. **data ownership** and the City's rights to access, extract, and republish its own data;
   d. **interoperability** and third-party or public integrations.
5. **Clariti Cloud Inc.'s proposal/response to RFP 24-11661-C**, specifically the sections
   addressing data access, APIs, reporting, and public/community-facing data.
6. The **RFP 24-11661-C solicitation document** and any addenda.
7. Any **staff evaluation, scoring sheets, or review-panel materials** that reference data access,
   open data, APIs, or public data provisions.

Please provide records in electronic format (searchable PDF or native) where available. If any
portion is claimed exempt, please cite the specific exemption and produce all reasonably
segregable non-exempt portions. I am happy to narrow scope in consultation if that speeds a
response.
