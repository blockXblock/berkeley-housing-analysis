**Subject: Public Records Act request — Corridors Zoning Update: parcel database, prioritization analysis, and feasibility work product (College, North Shattuck, Solano)**

Under the California Public Records Act (Gov. Code § 7920.000 et seq.), I request the following records
concerning the **Corridors Zoning Update** (consultant team led by Raimi + Associates; project areas: College
Avenue [Dwight to Alcatraz], North Shattuck, and Solano Avenue). This request includes records **prepared by,
used by, or in the possession of the City's consultants for this project on the City's behalf** (§ 7920.545), not
only records physically held by City staff.

**1. The parcel-level GIS database (existing conditions).** The dataset underlying the Existing Conditions
Report and the color-coded maps, provided as **a complete copy/export in its native format** — Esri **File
Geodatabase (.gdb)** where maintained as such — **and** in **Shapefile, GeoJSON, and CSV/Excel**, together with
the **data dictionary / field metadata**. Include, per parcel: APN, address, lot area; **existing zoning**;
**existing dwelling-unit count** and use/occupancy; existing building floor area and/or stories; and any
**historic-resource, rent-controlled, or UC/tax-exempt ownership** flags.

**2. The parcel prioritization / opportunity-site ("soft-site") analysis — the decision records.** The consultant
examined and ranked parcels for redevelopment potential; I request the records that document those decisions:
   a. the **scored/ranked parcel dataset** (each parcel's suitability/priority score, tier, or opportunity-site
      classification under each alternative), as native GIS + CSV/Excel;
   b. the **methodology** defining the scoring/selection **criteria and thresholds** (e.g., lot size,
      improvement-to-land-value ratio, underutilization, ownership, historic status) — memos, model
      documentation, or a technical appendix;
   c. any **"opportunity sites," "soft sites," or "sites inventory"** list/map/memo produced for the project.

**3. Capacity / yield model.** The model outputs giving **per-parcel or per-site projected unit counts, FAR,
height, and density** under each zoning alternative (native spreadsheet/model file, not just PDF summaries).

**4. Economic / financial-feasibility work product (Strategic Economics).** Any **pro forma, financial-feasibility,
or market study** evaluating whether development is feasible on the corridor parcels/sites — including site-level
assumptions and results — in native spreadsheet format.

**5. Draft regulatory text.** Draft **zoning-ordinance amendments, development-standards tables, and objective
design standards** for the three corridors, including any redline/track-changes and comparison-to-existing tables.

**6. Web maps / hosted GIS.** Any **ArcGIS Online web maps, StoryMaps, dashboards, or hosted feature layers**
created for the project, and the **underlying hosted data** (item export or service definition).

**7. Scope and deliverables.** The consultant **contract/scope of work and deliverables schedule** for the CZU
(so the full set of work product can be identified), and the **6th Cycle Housing Element sites inventory**
(2023–2031) as a parcel/site-level electronic file (APN/address, zoning, projected units by income tier).

Please provide records in **native electronic format** per § 7922.570 (databases and GIS exported in the format
in which they are maintained; do not flatten to PDF where a data file exists). If any portion is claimed exempt,
please cite the specific exemption and produce all reasonably segregable non-exempt portions (§ 7922.525). I am
happy to confer to narrow or sequence production — **highest priority: items 1 (the parcel database) and 2 (the
prioritization/soft-site decision records).**
