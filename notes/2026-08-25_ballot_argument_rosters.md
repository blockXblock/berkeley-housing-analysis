# November 2026 Berkeley Ballot Measures — Who Filed For and Against

**Prepared for the Vision 2050 working group · August 25, 2026**

Compiled from the complete set of 25 ballot arguments filed with the Berkeley City
Clerk (13 primary arguments, received Aug 12–14, 2026; 12 rebuttals, received
Aug 20–21, 2026), distributed by the Clerk's office via Dropbox to argument filers.
These documents are not yet posted on berkeleyca.gov. Titles are as filed.

---

## Measure U — $300M Infrastructure Bond

**YES** — author: Mayor Adena Ishii; joint campaign with Measure V (safevibrantberkeley.com)

- *Primary signers:* Adena Ishii (Mayor); Dr. Sharon Goldfarb (Co-President, League
  of Women Voters Berkeley/Albany/Emeryville); John Caner (Local Business Leader);
  Eric Weaver (Neighborhood Wildfire Safety Organizer); **Jenny Wong (Berkeley City
  Auditor)**
- *Rebuttal signers:* Ishii; Goldfarb; Henry C. Levy (Alameda County Treasurer);
  Weaver; Caner
- *Quoted in the rebuttal:* **Gordon Wozniak** ("Former Councilmember/Mayor's Vision
  2050 Task Force Member"): "Any suggestion that Vision 2050 recommendations are not
  the basis for Measure U, is false. Vote YES on U…"; Berkeley Fire Fighters IAFF 1227

**NO** — submitted on behalf of **Affordable Berkeley** (Isabelle Gaston, Assistant
Treasurer; the organization that opposed the 2022 Measure L bond)

- *Primary signers:* Isabelle Gaston; Donna DiDiemar (Neighborhood Activist); Michael
  Katz (Writer and Cyclist); Joshua Glatt (Software Engineer and Composer); Stephen
  Tracy (Retired Public School Teacher)
- *Rebuttal signers:* DiDiemar (Neighborhood Activist and Former Small Business
  Owner); Paul Mathew (Building Decarbonization Consultant); McKinley Crespo (Small
  Business Owner); Steve Robey (Retired IT Consultant)

*Note: the primary Argument Against praises Vision 2050 ("a thoughtful comprehensive
long-term strategy") and charges that its recommendations "have not been implemented
or meaningfully incorporated into Measure U." The Wozniak quote in the YES rebuttal
is the campaign's direct answer to that charge.*

## Patterns

- **The YES side is institutionally deep:** the Mayor authored three measures'
  arguments; a State Senator, the Alameda County Treasurer, the sitting City
  Auditor, councilmembers, and beneficiary organizations fill the rosters.
- **The organized NO side is one small organization** — Affordable Berkeley
  (roughly ten rotating individuals) covering U, V, and Z — **plus three
  independent actors:** the realtor/landlord coalition on X, David Denton on Y,
  and Damian Park on AA.
- **Vision 2050 is invoked by both sides of Measure U** — by the opposition as the
  plan the bond ignores, and by the campaign (through Gordon Wozniak's rebuttal
  quote) as the plan the bond is based on.
