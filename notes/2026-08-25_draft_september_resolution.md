# DRAFT — For Council Action, September 2026

## Resolution No. ______–N.S.
### Measure U Implementation Framework: Binding Commitments on Project Prioritization, Lifecycle Costing, Maintenance, and Oversight

**WHEREAS**, the Council has placed Measure U, a $300,000,000 general obligation
bond, on the November 3, 2026 ballot, and the City's own Tax Rate Statement
projects approximately $610,000,000 in total debt service, with final repayment
in Fiscal Year 2066–67; and

**WHEREAS**, Resolution 72,338-N.S. states only that bond funding "should be
guided by the City's non-exhaustive list of proposed projects," language that is
a non-binding recital and commits the City to nothing; and

**WHEREAS**, the City's identified unfunded capital need of approximately
$2 billion exceeds the bond authorization by nearly seven times, making
disciplined prioritization the difference between a plan and a list; and

**WHEREAS**, the City's adopted asset management policy requires that capital
decisions account for full lifecycle costs, and voters are entitled to know that
Measure U proceeds will be governed by that policy;

**NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED** that the Council adopts the following
commitments, which shall govern all expenditures of Measure U proceeds:

**1. Prioritization before issuance.** Before the first bond series is issued,
the Council shall adopt by resolution a ranked project list, ordered by published
criteria: life-safety and fire risk first, then asset condition, service impact,
geographic equity, and leverage of state and federal matching funds. No proceeds
shall be spent on a project not on the adopted ranked list or added to it by the
same public process.

**2. Lifecycle cost analysis for every project.** No project shall receive bond
funding until the City Manager publishes, and the Council accepts, a lifecycle
cost analysis covering thirty years of operation, maintenance, and component
renewal, consistent with the City's asset management policy.

**3. Debt term matched to asset life.** Bond proceeds shall not finance assets or
components whose expected useful life is shorter than the term of the bonds that
finance them. Short-lived components shall be funded from operating or other
appropriate sources.

**4. Maintenance funded before construction.** No construction appropriation
shall be approved until the project's recurring operating and maintenance cost is
quantified and a funding source identified. The City Manager shall report
annually on the aggregate new O&M obligation created by completed bond projects.

**5. Oversight with authority.** The citizens' oversight committee shall be
independent of the City Manager, shall review and comment publicly on each bond
series before issuance, shall publish an annual report comparing expenditures and
schedules to the adopted ranked list, and may refer any matter directly to the
City Auditor. The City Auditor shall audit each bond series.

**6. Public reporting.** The City shall maintain a public, per-project dashboard
showing budget, expenditures, schedule, and change orders, updated at least
quarterly.

**7. Binding effect.** These commitments govern all Measure U expenditures and
may be amended only by a two-thirds vote of the Council following a noticed
public hearing.
