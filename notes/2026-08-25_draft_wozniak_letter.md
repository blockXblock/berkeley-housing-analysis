# DRAFT — To Gordon Wozniak, from the Vision 2050 working group

Dear Gordon,

We are writing about the statement attributed to you in the City's filed rebuttal
for Measure U — "Any suggestion that Vision 2050 recommendations are not the
basis for Measure U, is false" — signed under the title "Mayor's Vision 2050 Task
Force Member." We ask you to withdraw it, or amend it, before the public
examination period closes and the Voter Information Guide goes to print. That
window closes in days, which is why we are raising this now and directly.

Three reasons.

**First, the statement is not accurate, and our own published work is the
record.** This group's analysis — which you helped author — documents precisely
how Measure U relates to Vision 2050: it funds a portion of what Vision 2050
recommended, and it omits what Vision 2050 insisted on: lifecycle costing,
prioritization of a capital program against a $2 billion need, and maintenance
funding for what gets built. "Partly based on" is defensible. "Any suggestion
otherwise is false" is not — and the suggestion it calls false includes findings
we published together under our own names.

**Second, the title conscripts Vision 2050 itself.** The quote does not speak as
Gordon Wozniak, private citizen — every voter who reads it will hear Vision 2050
speaking. Hundreds of residents contributed volunteer years to that work. None of
them were asked. The group has taken a deliberate, publicly stated position: we
are independent advisors, affiliated with no campaign, evaluating Measure U on
the evidence. Your quote, under that title, converts the group's name into a
campaign asset and makes our stated independence untrue on its face.

**Third, withdrawing it strengthens the cause you are trying to help.** Both
campaigns are now claiming Vision 2050 — the opposition says the bond ignores it;
your quote says the bond is built on it. What persuades the undecided voter is
neither absolute. It is the credible, independent finding we are preparing to
publish: that Measure U is worth supporting if the Council binds itself to the
priorities, lifecycle discipline, and real oversight that Vision 2050 called for.
That finding is worth far more to Measure U than one more endorsement — but only
if the group's independence is intact when we publish it. If the quote stands, we
will be obliged to correct it publicly, and a public contradiction between you
and your own group serves no one but the opposition.

The mechanics are simple and honorable: a short letter from you to the City
Clerk and to the rebuttal's author withdrawing consent for the use of your name
and the quotation, or amending the statement to one that is accurate — for
example, that Vision 2050's work informed the bond's project list. We would
welcome either.

We value what you have given this effort over many years, and we are asking as
colleagues: take Vision 2050's name off the campaign's absolute, and stand with
us on the independent ground we all agreed to hold.

With respect,

[names]
