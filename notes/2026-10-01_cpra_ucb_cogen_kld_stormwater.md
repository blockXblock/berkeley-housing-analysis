# Three records requests (drafted 2026-10-01, not sent)

Each names records the agency already keeps under its own name (the tactic that got #26-1368 and #26-1971 filled in days).
Each fills a gap the Berkeley Snapshot report states plainly (`docs/berkeley-snapshot-2026-10.html`).

## 1. UC Berkeley — the cogeneration plant and the STARS energy entry

**To:** UC Berkeley Public Records Office, **pra@berkeley.edu** (or by mail: Public Records Office, University of
California, Berkeley, 200 California Hall, MC #1500, Berkeley, CA 94720-1500). UC is subject to the California Public Records
Act; the campus must say within 10 days (24 if extended) whether the records will be released. Source:
chancellor.berkeley.edu/faq/public-records-act-requests (checked 2026-10-01).
**Why:** The campus's 2025 STARS report (credit OP-5) gives 2023 energy use as 78,414 MWh, including 30,014 MWh of natural
gas, while its emissions credit (OP-6) reports 134,957 t CO2e from stationary combustion, which implies about 746,000 MWh
of gas. The campus's total electricity is reported nowhere, because the plant's own generation is not published.

> Under the California Public Records Act, I request copies of the following records of the University of California,
> Berkeley:
>
> 1. For the campus central cogeneration plant, for each calendar year 2019 through 2025: (a) natural gas consumed
>    (therms or MMBtu); (b) electricity generated (MWh); (c) steam produced (pounds or MMBtu); (d) electricity purchased
>    from the grid for the campus (MWh); and (e) electricity exported, if any. Records such as monthly or annual plant
>    operating reports, utility meter summaries, or the data submitted for the campus greenhouse-gas inventory to The
>    Climate Registry or the California Air Resources Board would satisfy this request.
> 2. The data files, calculations and notes used to complete AASHE STARS credit **OP-5 (Energy Use)** in the campus's
>    STARS report submitted February 19, 2025, including the source of the natural-gas figure (30,014 MWh) and any record of
>    which facilities' gas use it covers.
> 3. The campus's most recent mandatory greenhouse-gas report to the California Air Resources Board (Regulation for the
>    Mandatory Reporting of Greenhouse Gas Emissions), including the fuel-use data for the cogeneration facility.
> 4. Any report or memorandum since January 1, 2024 stating the schedule for decommissioning the cogeneration plant under
>    the Berkeley Clean Energy Campus project, including the report cited by the STARS reviewer giving completion in 2033.
>
> Electronic copies, in their native format where they exist as spreadsheets, are preferred. If any record is withheld,
> please identify it and the exemption claimed. Thank you.

**Note:** item 3 may also be obtainable without a request: CARB publishes facility-level mandatory-reporting data annually
(search the CARB MRR data release for the UC Berkeley facility). Check that first; if it gives the plant's fuel use, drop
item 3.

## 2. City of Berkeley — the evacuation model's road-by-road output

**To:** City of Berkeley (NextRequest: cityofberkeleyca.nextrequest.com). **Department:** Fire Department / Office of
Emergency Services.
**Why:** The City's July 2025 Evacuation Time Estimate Study (KLD Associates, TR-1296) maps congested roads ("a thick red
line" for Level of Service F) and names some of them, but never counts them. The model's link-level output answers "how
many streets jam, and for how long" directly.

> Under the California Public Records Act, I request copies of the following records related to the City of Berkeley
> Evacuation Time Estimate Study prepared by KLD Associates, Inc. (KLD TR-1296, Final Report Rev. 0, July 1, 2025):
>
> 1. The traffic simulation model's link-level output for each evacuation region and scenario reported in the study's Table
>    7-4, in electronic form: for each road link, its identifier, street name, capacity, and the Level of Service (or
>    volume-to-capacity ratio, speed, or queue length) at each reported time interval after the evacuation order.
> 2. The road network file used in the model (link and node attributes, including lane counts, capacity and free-flow
>    speed), in its native or an exportable format (for example shapefile, GeoJSON or CSV).
> 3. Any appendix, data deliverable or technical memorandum delivered by KLD to the City under the contract approved March
>    14, 2023 (consent calendar item 04) that is not included in the published report, including the analysis of Access
>    Impaired Neighborhoods.
> 4. The deliverables list or scope of work in that contract identifying the electronic files KLD was to provide.
>
> Electronic copies in their native formats are preferred. If any record is withheld, please identify it and the
> exemption claimed. Thank you.

## 3. City of Berkeley — the Stormwater Master Plan

**To:** City of Berkeley (NextRequest). **Department:** Public Works (Engineering / Watershed).
**Why:** The City's 2024 Local Hazard Mitigation Plan said the Stormwater Master Plan was "expected to be completed at the
end of 2025." The City's page still shows it in planning, with community meetings in fall 2026, and no capital-needs figure
has been published. The storm drains were designed for a 10-year storm; many parts are over 90 years old.

> Under the California Public Records Act, I request copies of the following records concerning the City of Berkeley
> Stormwater Master Plan:
>
> 1. The executed consultant contract (and any amendments) for the Stormwater Master Plan, including the scope of work
>    and schedule of deliverables.
> 2. All deliverables received from the consultant to date, including any draft plan chapters, condition assessments,
>    hydraulic model results, capacity-deficiency lists, and capital improvement project lists or cost estimates.
> 3. The current project schedule, and any record explaining a change from the completion date of the end of 2025 stated
>    in the City's 2024 Local Hazard Mitigation Plan.
> 4. Any estimate, since January 1, 2020, of the total cost to bring the storm drain system to its design standard or to a
>    proposed new standard, including any update to the 2011 Watershed Management Plan's project costs.
> 5. The storm drain asset inventory or condition data maintained by Public Works (for example, the GIS layer with pipe
>    age, material, diameter and condition rating), in electronic form.
>
> Electronic copies in their native formats are preferred. If any record is withheld, please identify it and the exemption
> claimed. Thank you.
