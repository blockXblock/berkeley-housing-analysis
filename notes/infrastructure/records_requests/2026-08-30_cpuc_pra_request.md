# Public Records Act request — CPUC: pole and transformer records for Berkeley

**Status:** DRAFT, not sent. John reviews and sends.

## Framing (important)

The CPUC "is not required to prepare new records..., or to compile, synthesize,
summarize, or index information or records in a form that does not exist at the time
of the request." So this request asks **only for records the CPUC already holds**, and
names them specifically. Asking for "all transformer locations in Berkeley" would be
correctly refused; asking for a filed report would not.

Note also that the pole databases created under **D.21-10-019** are held by the pole
owners, not the CPUC, and that decision expressly does not make them public. Do not
frame the request as seeking those databases.

## Records requested

**1. GO 165 compliance records for PG&E**
   General Order 165 requires records specifying, for each inspection, the circuit,
   area, facility or equipment inspected, the inspector, the date, any problems
   identified, and the scheduled date of corrective action. We request, for the
   period 2015–present:
   a. PG&E's Annual Inspection Reports filed under GO 165 (these appear to be
      published at `files.cpuc.ca.gov/ESRB_Audits/GO165/` through ~2014 — we request
      the years since).
   b. Any Safety and Enforcement Division audit, citation, or workpapers concerning
      PG&E distribution facility inspections in Alameda County or PG&E's East Bay
      division, including the underlying data tables.

**2. Wildfire Mitigation Plan underlying data**
   The equipment counts and risk tables supporting PG&E's Wildfire Mitigation Plan
   filings (currently the 2026–2028 WMP), specifically any table reporting counts of
   **distribution line transformers**, poles, and non-exempt equipment by division,
   district, or circuit — including any version submitted to the Office of Energy
   Infrastructure Safety and held by the Commission.

**3. Pole and conduit proceeding record**
   Non-confidential data submissions, workshop materials, and party workpapers in
   **I.17-06-027 / R.17-06-028** and the implementation of **D.21-10-019** that report
   pole counts or pole characteristics by county or municipality.

**4. GRC asset counts**
   From PG&E's General Rate Case filings (most recently **A.25-05-009**, the 2027 GRC),
   the exhibits, workpapers, or data-request responses reporting **distribution
   transformer unit counts and age distribution** — system-wide and, if reported at
   that granularity, by division or district.
